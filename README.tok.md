lipu NTARI tawa jan pi pali ilo (NTARI Developer Portal) li poki lipu lawa pi lipu sona. kulupu Network Theory Applied Research Institute (NTARI) li awen pona e ona.

lon tenpo kama la, poki lipu ni li kama poki lawa pi lipu sona NTARI tawa jan pi pali ilo. lipu sona ni li jo e nasin pi pali ilo lon tenpo kama, e lipu pi nasin ilo suli, e sitelen lili pi nasin pali, e lipu sona pi pali kulupu, e ijo pona tawa jan pi pana pali. tenpo ni la, jan li awen pona e lipu ni lon poki lipu "GitHub". sitelen "Markdown" li nasin nanpa wan ona. lon tenpo kama la, jan li ken pana e ona kepeken ilo pi lipu sona taso, sama ilo "Docusaurus".

## pali pi tenpo ni

tenpo ni la, poki lipu ni li toki e nasin open pi lawa e nasin open pi pana pali tawa lipu NTARI tawa jan pi pali ilo. jan li pali ala e lipu pi lipu sona lon tenpo ni.

## jan pi awen pona

kulupu NTARI li awen pona e lipu sona lon poki lipu ni.

## pana pali

sina ken pana e pali tawa lipu sona kepeken "issue" en "pull request" lon ilo "GitHub". pana sina li pona tawa mi mute. o lukin e lipu [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) lon nanpa wan. sina pini e ni la, o toki e ante sina.

## nasin pona pi kulupu

nasin "Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct" li lawa e pali jan lon poki lipu ni. o lukin e [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## awen pona en pakala pi poki lipu

sina wile toki e pakala lon lipu sona, anu e ike lon nasin pi poki lipu, anu e ken ike pi awen pona la, o lukin e [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
